# Where these rules come from

Our own summary, not quotes.

- Martin Jonasson and Petri Purho, "Juice it or lose it" (2012). They take a plain Breakout clone and add colour, tweening and easing, squash and stretch, particles, screen shake and sound one at a time. Their point: a game should answer every action, with as much response as possible for as little input as possible. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg
- Jan Willem Nijman (Vlambeer), "The art of screenshake" (2013). Many small changes to an action game, among them screen shake, hit pauses, knockback and making the camera react, each adding to how forceful actions feel. https://gamedeveloper.com/design/vlambeer-co-founder-shares-advice-on-building-better-action-games
